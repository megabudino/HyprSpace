## Codebase Patterns
- Accessibility helpers live in `Sources/AppBundle/util/accessibility.swift` and wrap AXUIElementCopyAttributeValue.
- AX observer callbacks can do synchronous AXUIElement work before dispatching the main refresh task.
- AX subrole comparisons can use literal strings like "AXDialog" when constants are unavailable.
- AxWindowKindTest fixtures live under `axDumps/` at repo root.
- Config tests read `docs/config-examples/i3-like-config-example.toml`.
